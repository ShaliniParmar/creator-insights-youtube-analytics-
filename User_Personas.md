 User Personas

 Persona 1: Small YouTube Creator

Name: Aisha
Age: 22
Role: Content Creator
Channel Stage: Early-stage creator

 Goals
* Increase video views
* Understand what type of content performs better
* Improve audience engagement
* Grow subscribers consistently

 Pain Points
* Difficulty understanding analytics
* Limited knowledge of data analysis
* Unsure which videos to repeat or improve
* Difficulty identifying the best upload time

 Needs from Creator Insights
* Simple KPI dashboard
* Category-wise performance comparison
* Upload-time analysis
* Top-performing video identification

 Persona 2: Growing YouTube Creator

Name: Rohan
Age: 27
Role: Full-time Content Creator
Channel Stage: Growing channel

 Goals
* Improve content strategy
* Increase watch time and subscriber growth
* Identify patterns in successful videos
* Make decisions based on historical performance

 Pain Points
* Large amount of performance data
* Difficult to compare videos manually
* Unclear relationship between engagement and growth
* Time-consuming analysis

 Needs from Creator Insights
* Interactive filters
* Video comparison
* Engagement and retention analysis
* Subscriber-growth insights
* Performance trends

Persona 3: Social Media Manager

Name: Neha
Age: 30
Role: Social Media / Content Manager

 Goals
* Monitor multiple content performance indicators
* Identify successful content patterns
* Support content planning decisions
* Present performance insights to stakeholders

 Pain Points
* Data spread across different reports
* Manual reporting takes time
* Difficult to identify actionable trends quickly
* Need for clear and presentable insights

 Needs from Creator Insights
* Centralized dashboard
* Clear KPIs
* Category and time-based analysis
* Easy-to-understand visual insights
* Data-driven content recommendations
