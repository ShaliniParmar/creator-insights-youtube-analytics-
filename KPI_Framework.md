KPI Framework

1. KPI Objective
The KPI framework defines the key metrics used to evaluate YouTube content performance and support data-driven content decisions.

2. Primary KPIs

| KPI                | Definition                                | Business Purpose                                    |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Total Views        | Total views generated by videos           | Measures overall content reach                      |
| Total Likes        | Total likes received                      | Measures positive audience engagement               |
| Total Comments     | Total comments received                   | Measures audience interaction                       |
| Watch Hours        | Total hours watched                       | Measures content consumption                        |
| Subscribers Gained | Subscribers gained from videos            | Measures contribution to channel growth             |
| CTR (%)            | Click-through rate                        | Measures how effectively content attracts clicks    |
| Retention (%)      | Percentage of content retained by viewers | Indicates how well content holds audience attention |

 3. Secondary Metrics

| Metric                     | Purpose                                          |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Video Count                | Measures publishing volume                       |
| Average Views per Video    | Compares average video reach                     |
| Average Likes per Video    | Evaluates average engagement                     |
| Average Comments per Video | Evaluates average interaction                    |
| Average CTR                | Provides an overall click-through benchmark      |
| Average Retention          | Provides an overall audience-retention benchmark |
| Average Subscribers Gained | Compares average subscriber contribution         |

 4. KPI Dimensions

KPIs can be analyzed across:
* Category — Compare performance across content categories
* Upload Hour — Identify performance patterns by publishing time
* Publish Date — Analyze performance across time
* Video — Identify individual high and low performers

5. KPI Decision Framework
Reach
Use Views and Video Count to understand content reach and publishing volume.
Engagement
Use Likes and Comments to understand audience interaction.
Audience Attention
Use CTR and Retention to evaluate how effectively content attracts and holds viewers.
Channel Growth
Use Subscribers Gained to identify content contributing to audience growth.
Content Consumption
Use Watch Hours to understand overall viewing activity.

6. KPI Usage
The KPIs should not be evaluated individually. Combining multiple metrics provides a more meaningful view of content performance.

For example:
High Views + High Retention + High Subscribers Gained
→ Potentially strong content worth studying for future content strategy.

High Views + Low Retention
→ Content attracts viewers but may need improvement in audience retention.

Low Views + High Retention
→ Content may have strong audience response but weaker reach/discovery.

7. KPI Data Source
The KPI framework is based on the YouTube Creator Dataset used in this project.
Relevant fields include:

* views
* likes
* comments
* ctr_pct
* retention_pct
* watch_hours
* subscribers_gained
* category
* upload_hour
* publish_date
