RICE Prioritization

Objective
RICE prioritization is used to identify which product features should be developed first.

RICE Formula
RICE Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort

Feature Prioritization

| Feature                         | Reach | Impact | Confidence | Effort | RICE Score | Priority |
| ------------------------------- | ----: | -----: | ---------: | -----: | ---------: | -------- |
| Performance Dashboard           |   100 |      3 |        0.9 |      2 |        135 | High     |
| Category Analysis               |    90 |      3 |        0.9 |      2 |      121.5 | High     |
| Upload Time Analysis            |    80 |      2 |        0.8 |      2 |         64 | High     |
| Subscriber Growth Analysis      |    75 |      3 |        0.8 |      3 |         60 | High     |
| Engagement & Retention Analysis |    85 |      2 |        0.8 |      3 |       45.3 | Medium   |
| Automated Insights              |    70 |      3 |        0.7 |      5 |       29.4 | Medium   |
| Best Upload Time Prediction     |    60 |      3 |        0.6 |      6 |         18 | Low      |
| AI Content Recommendations      |    50 |      3 |        0.5 |      8 |        9.4 | Low      |

Priority Decisions

High Priority — MVP
These features provide immediate value and require relatively lower effort:
* Performance Dashboard
* Category Analysis
* Upload Time Analysis
* Subscriber Growth Analysis

Medium Priority
These features can be introduced after the core MVP:
* Engagement & Retention Analysis
* Automated Insights

Low Priority — Future Releases
These features require more data, development effort, or advanced capabilities:
* Best Upload Time Prediction
* AI Content Recommendations

Product Decision
The MVP should first focus on helping creators understand their existing data.
The product development approach is:
MVP Analytics → Advanced Insights → Predictive Features → AI Recommendations**
This prioritization ensures that high-value and lower-effort features are delivered before more complex features.
